Private by default
On Android, tap the file chooser and select Gallery, Photos, or Files. Uploads are sent privately to your connected YouTube channel.

Uploads remain private. If you choose a future time, YouTube’s native scheduled publishing is requested; availability depends on channel eligibility and API response. Confirm before submitting.
